Why the name: 'GrowthToDo'?

There is plenty that goes into building a business. Strategy, vision, positioning, timing. All of it is real. None of it is optional.

Most things can be framed as a growth problem - personal growth, personnel growth, a growth in mindset, a growth in the quality of thinking. Even a decision to do a strategic slow, or downsize is a growth problem in a large enough time scale.

No matter how complex the problem or the goal, everything ultimately reduces to a good to-do list. Getting a business to product-market fit is a to-do list. Launching a product is a to-do list. Getting to Mars is a todo list. The list might have a million items. It is still a list.

Breaking problems down into parts and then into a sequenced to do list that you iterate on is the problem half solved.

The question finally becomes: do you have the right items on the list, are you doing them in the right order, and are you moving at the speed the situation actually demands? Are you doing Growth ToDos right?

Right to-dos. Right order. Right speed.

Most of the time when a business is stuck, one of those three is off. Wrong items on the list. Right items in the wrong order. Or right items, right order, moving too slow for the window that exists.

Hence, GrowthToDo
